# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

One Spanish-speaking operator managing the NewSphere YouTube channel locally.

## Product Purpose

Prepare and upload one researched evergreen video each day, with seven weekday niches and a visible queue of work that still needs attention.

## Operating Context

Authenticated local Windows application, PostgreSQL, persistent media and Data Protection keys. NotebookLM is the exclusive video generator. Personal NotebookLM generation and download use the official interface; YouTube uses authorized OAuth and the official API.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve existing editorial, research, source package, manual task, media inspection, review, upload, scheduling, history and recovery workflows. Never imply that planned work has been generated or uploaded. The first authorized real upload is private. Public visibility requires separate authorization.

## Product Principles

- Show the next actionable task and its real blocker.
- Keep configuration together and advanced settings out of the daily path.
- Preserve evidence, source attribution and immutable history.
- Reduce operator work without inventing provider capabilities.

## Design Delegation

The operator explicitly delegated configuration and visual/UX decisions and asked to proceed without further preference questions. Proposed default: one daily slot at 18:00 Europe/Madrid; future public visibility remains undecided.
